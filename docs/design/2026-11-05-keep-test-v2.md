## For the owner

Today a new player is kept only if it ends more floors up on average. The first player that learned card choices from human runs
beats the second boss twice as often as the current one (107 against 49 times in 2,000 games) but dies at the first boss a little
more often, so it averages 0.3 floors less, and the test refuses it. The new test keeps a player when it beats more of the bosses that
the current player rarely beats (the second boss now, the third later), or when it goes further on average as before, and in both
cases it may not lose more than about one floor elsewhere. It is like judging a climber by the highest camp reached, not by the
average height of every attempt. Under it that player is kept and every earlier verdict stands; since the copy of the game is less
exact in Act 2, the next night's real games must confirm the gain.

**Why floors are the wrong primary here.** A run that beats the Act 1 boss ends 13.5 floors further than one that does not (29.3
against 15.8 in generation 11's arm); one that also beats the Act 2 boss ends 12.9 further again (42.2). Floors weigh the two kills
alike, so trading 3.9 Act 1 kills for 2.9 Act 2 kills reads as a loss, although the Act 2 boss holds 80% of the gap to humans (memo 2.1)
and the candidate beats it 11.1 times in 100 when it has beaten the Act 1 boss, against 4.7. The Act 2 kill per 100 *runs* is
unconditional: every Act 1 death is already in it.

**Why Act 2 alone is the wrong primary too.** Generations 9 and 11 were Act 1 gains (B1 +4.95 and +8.25 points, floors +1.04 and +1.07,
B2 flat: 42/45 and 54/40); an Act 2 primary would have refused both, and the fight half's next steps (stakes x16 at bosses, memo
milestone 7) gain mostly at the Act 1 boss. So v2 has two routes.

## 2. The rule

### 2.1 Two routes, one alpha

Per run, the bosses beaten: B1, B2, B3a, B3b (the win). From `boss_damage_acts` (first entry per act, fraction exactly 1.0; a win beats
all four); a row without the field (blocks before 2026-10-25) uses the floor cuts 17 / 33 / 48. The **frontier** f (2.2) is a boss;
**deep_f** of a run = how many of the bosses from f on it beats, paired per seed, in points per 100 runs. Today (f = B2) that is B2 + B3a +
win: the memo's headline, plus any deeper boss, which only adds.

- **Route A, deeper:** the paired deep_f gain has one-sided p < 0.005 (cluster bootstrap lower bound at 0.005 above 0, the floor
  primary's machinery) **and** a point gain of at least +1.0 per 100 **and** the floor guard: the paired mean floor's one-sided 95%
  lower bound is at least -1.0 floors.
- **Route F, further:** today's floor primary at one-sided 0.005 (lower bound above 0) **and** deep_f not worse: its one-sided 95%
  upper bound is not below 0.
- **Promote** when route A or route F holds and every guard of section 3 holds; wins stay a co-primary once both arms win 10 in 100.
  Under no change at all P(promote) <= 0.005 + 0.005, plus 0.001 for the funnel's early looks (4): 0.011, today's figure. The split costs
  no past promotion: the weakest floor promotion in the ledger had z 3.0 (E3-r1), above 2.58.
- **Minimum gain** +1.0 per 100: the smallest step that counts toward the go bar (8 per 100 from about 2.5). At 2,000 seeds it never
  binds (significance already needs about +1.5); it stops a cheap rented 6,000-seed block promoting a +0.6.

### 2.2 The frontier, and the ladder

f = the deepest boss the incumbent beats in at least **1.5 of 100 runs**, pooled over its own rows on the last three ledger blocks it
played (as base or as the promoted candidate), B1 when none; fixed before a block is played. At 1.5 per 100 a 2,000-seed block catches a
doubling of that boss's kills 8 times in 10 (discordance about 2.6 p, z = (769 p)^0.5). Generation 11's lineage: B2 2.0 to 2.3 per 100,
B3a 0.0 to 0.1, so f = B2 now (B1 up to E9, lineage B2 rate 1.2; B2 from E10). **The ladder:** f moves to B3a when the incumbent beats the
first Act 3 boss 1.5 times in 100 (30 per block), then to the win, automatically and recorded; deep_f counts every later boss, route F
keeps rewarding gains before f, and wins keep D24's co-primary clause at 10 in 100.

### 2.3 What a block resolves

Paired SE of B2 kills at 2,000 seeds: 0.58 points on l05's block (deep_B2: 0.63). Model (paired, one fifth of the base's kills shared, as
on that block: 10 of 49), seeds for 80% power at one-sided 0.005:

| base B2 per 100 | +1 point | +2 points | +3 points | SE at 2,000, +2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.45 (gen 11 on the block) | 5,700 | 1,700 | 890 | 0.54 |
| 5.35 (l05) | 11,200 | 3,100 | 1,500 | 0.73 |
| 10 | 19,800 | 5,200 | 2,500 | 0.95 |

So 2,000 seeds resolve about +1.8 points against generation 11 and +2.5 against l05; **a +1 point gain needs 5,700 to 11,200 seeds**.
Recommendation: 2,000 seeds while the incumbent's frontier rate is under 5 per 100, then 3,000 (l05 is at 5.35: +2 points at about 80%;
the funnel's early stops cut the mean cost, section 4; a rented 3,000-seed block is about $1.5, memo 4). +20% at 10 per 100 needs 5,200.

## 3. Guards

| guard | what it measures today | v2 |
|---|---|---|
| floor primary | paired mean floor, 99% lower bound > 0 | route F at 0.005; in route A a non-inferiority guard: 95% lower >= -1.0 floor |
| Act 1 kill guard | B1 not worse: exact McNemar on discordant pairs, fires at one-sided 0.05 | **reported only** (`act1_review`; blocks nothing, early promotion included) |
| damage guard | paired Act 1 boss damage fraction (0 if not reached), fires when its 95% upper bound < 0 | **reported only**, with the Act 2 boss's |
| drift | candidate's B1 rate (Wilson 95%) wholly below the lineage's last three blocks pooled: flag; two in a row: block | **kept as is** |
| attempt cap | two candidates per generation and recipe | kept |
| crash | none (a crash is a floor-0 loss) | **new:** candidate's crashed or lost seeds at most base's + max(2, 0.25% of the block) |
| wins | co-primary at 10 in 100 both arms | kept |
| night | none | **new, route A only:** the next night confirms (below) |

**The floor margin, 1.0 floor at 95%.** One typical promotion: the promoted blocks since generation 6 gained +1.72, +0.75, +2.04, +1.04,
+1.07 (median 1.07). On l05's exchange (0.11 floors lost per Act 2 point gained) it admits about 6.5 points of Act 2 gain, the whole road
to the go bar, but not a collapse: generation 12's -5.91 and the pure imitation's -3.14 fail it at once. At 2,000 seeds (SE 0.20) a point
loss up to about 0.68 passes. A sliding margin (so much floor per Act 2 point) was rejected: it couples the guard to the primary's noise
and adds a knob; the drift check bounds the cumulative loss. **The verdict on l05 turns on this number:** at 0.5 floor it fails (lower
bound -0.63).

**No Act 1 kill guard.** Route A's measure is unconditional, so every Act 1 death is already charged to it; a separate Act 1 guard forbids
exactly the human trade (humans reach the Act 1 boss in 74 of 100 runs against our 90, and beat the Act 2 boss six times as often).
What bounds Act 1 erosion: the floor margin per block and the unchanged drift check across blocks (l05's 48.25 in 100, Wilson upper 50.4,
against the lineage's pooled lower bound 49.7: clear; a second step like it would be flagged, a third blocked). The damage guard is the
kill guard in finer form; Act 2 boss damage is a weaker signal than Act 2 kills (paired z +3.06 against +4.9), so both are reported only.

**The night confirms a route A promotion** while the copy is less exact in Act 2 than in Act 1 (4.4 against 0.66 disagreements per 1,000
decisions, memo 2.2 item 6; floors agree within noise, so route F is not confirmed). The promotion night plays the new incumbent on 2,000
real-game runs (`--runs-promoted`): confirmed when its Act 2 boss kills per 100 runs beat the previous incumbent's pooled real-game rate
(one-sided 95% lower bound of the unpaired difference above 0) and its mean floor is not below that pool's by more than 1.0 at 95%.
Generation 11's pool, nights 11-03 to 11-06: 119 in 3,500 runs, **3.40 per 100**, floor 23.33. If l05's copy ratio (2.2x) holds in the game
(7.4) the check has z about 6, at half the gain (5.1) about 2.9. On a failure the orchestrator restores the previous `current.json` and
records it in DECISIONS; the attempt stays spent.

**The exam (D38, D55).** The rule reads two arms' `runs.jsonl` on a fresh E-block, the frontier the incumbent's own arms, the confirmation
real-game nights: no human run, no fifth. The exam fifth is an input to nothing here (rule, funnel, frontier, margin, any choice in this
doc); its agreement score (memo 3.2) is computed once per frozen candidate beside `promotion.json` as a report, never a check.

## 4. The funnel

Looks as today (200, 500, then every 300 to the block's end); the per-look statistics are normal (`seq_stats`) on both the floor and
deep_f differences. At 200 seeds the arms hold about 5 and 11 Act 2 kills, so the smoke look judges the floor and only the sign of deep_f:

- **Smoke (200), refuse** when the floor mean is below -1.0 and deep_f's mean is not above 0, or the floor's one-sided 95% upper bound is
  below -1.0 (no route can pass).
- **Later looks, refuse** when route F is dead (floor 99% upper bound below 0) **and** route A is dead (floor 99% upper below -1.0, or
  deep_f's one-sided 95% upper bound below +1.0).
- **Early promote** (looks 2 to L-1) when a route's z reaches 3.72 (0.001 split over five interim looks and two routes) and that route's
  other conditions hold on the seeds played, the drift check is not flagged, and the crash and attempt checks hold. Last look: 2.1.

|---|---|---|
| l05, 20,000 random 200-seed subsets: refused at the smoke | **11.9%** | **2.4%** |
| l05, 20,000 random 500-seed subsets: refused | 4.1% | 0.0% |

So today's smoke would have refused l05 about one time in eight; v2's about one in forty. The early-promote bar is strict (3.72) and the

## 7. Caveats

  beating the Act 2 boss) and miss B3a kills on floor 48 (A10 has two Act 3 bosses); rows with the field use it. **Unverified:** the
  confirmation's power in the game (assumes the copy's ratio); the size table's shared-kill share at other rates.
