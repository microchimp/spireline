## What we know

The run brain (the "run value", `run_value.pt` / `.npz` / `.onnx` in every `referee/agent/gen*/candidate-*/`) scores a mid-run state; the run player searches over imagined futures and compares them by it. Analyst A found it is fitted to a floor target in which a win is worth about four floors more than dying at the last boss, so the brain barely cares about winning. Of generation 17's 52,824 plain runs, 12,048 got past the Act 2 boss, 7,797 reached the first Act 3 boss, 1,227 got past it, 54 won. Humans (public corpus, aggregates): past the Act 2 boss 29 in 100, then past the first Act 3 boss 71 in 100 per arrival, then win 62 in 100; ours 23, 16 and 4.5.

## Questions the design must answer

1. What the brain predicts today, exactly: the target's definition, the loss, the features, where "a win is worth four floors" comes from, and what the search does with the scalar (how a mid-run value is mixed with fight outcomes, and why the scale matters).
2. The proposed target: per-boss survival heads (past the Act 1 boss; past the Act 2 boss given past Act 1; past the first Act 3 boss given past Act 2; win given past the first Act 3 boss) chained into a win chance, and a second scalar "bosses beaten from Act 2 on". Say how a mid-run state gets its value from these heads, how the magnitudes compare across acts, and what scalar the search consumes (expected bosses beaten from here; log-odds of winning; a blend). Prefer the smallest change to the player that lets the search see the win.
3. The data for the last heads: 1,227 past the first Act 3 boss and 54 wins in generation 17 alone; pooled over generations 15 to 18 and the judged arms, how many; what shrinkage or pooling keeps the last head from fitting noise; no human corpus data in the fit (rules in COMMON.md).
4. The risk A names: the refused refits traded Act 1 survival for later floors. Which refits, by how much (HANDOFF and `referee/agent/gen*/candidate-*/promotion*.json`), and what in the design or the keep rule (v3's collapse guard) limits it.
5. The recipe: fit time on the card (hours), the venv (`D:/spireline/venv-gpu/Scripts/python.exe`), the matched-window rule (`fit_run_value_matched`), the keep checks a new value fit must pass (`vrun_keep_*`), the candidate folder's files (which are copied from the champion, which are new), the marker the lab's train job expects (name, fields; the lab now judges under rule v3), and a smoke test that tells within an hour that the fit is sane (for example: calibration of the predicted chance of getting past the first Act 3 boss on held-out generation 18 runs, by decile).
