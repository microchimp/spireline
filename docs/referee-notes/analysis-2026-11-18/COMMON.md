## The project in one paragraph

Spireline is an attempt to build an AI that beats the best human Slay the Spire 2 players at Ascension 10, Ironclad first. It plays on our own copy of the game (a fork of an open-source re-implementation, `D:/spireline/fork/sts2`, checked against the real game move by move) because the real game cannot be forked or sped up. The run player searches over imagined futures with a learned "run value" network fitted on the outcomes of the champion's own runs (Monte Carlo value learning; the owner's D6/D7 forbid end-to-end reinforcement learning and dense hand-shaped rewards; D60 allows a policy fit on outcomes for route, card and rest choices), a fight brain trained in a separate "fight loop" on fight positions and examined against the humans' own 400 fights, a human prior from a public corpus of human runs, and a "student" network. A lab (`D:/spireline/referee/LAB`, a job runner) plays the champion's plain runs around the clock as data, judges each candidate against the champion on 2,000 paired seeds under a keep rule (v1, v2, v3 since 2026-11-18 16:08Z), and promotes. Eighteen generations have been promoted since 2026-10-22. Real-game nights (2,000 runs of the champion in the real game, scored) ran until 2026-11-06 and not since.
