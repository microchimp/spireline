# Lane 14: the fight loop's gate, rule v5

Written 2026-11-21 by lane 14 (Opus 5.5), after the fresh-draw check (`gate-v5/v4-fresh-check.md`: v4's promotion holds, +1.94 points on fresh draws, 95% lower bound +0.90). Decision: D68 addendum 5 decision 4. Words once: the **fight loop** trains the fight brain; its **gate** decides whether a new brain (a checkpoint) replaces the current one (the **incumbent**); the **ruler** is the fixed set of fight starts the gate plays; a **draw** is one replay of a start with its own fixed seeds, shared by both players; a **route** is one way to pass; an **era** is the stretch between two promotions.

## 1. Why a new rule

The sceptic review (11-21, sections 3 and 7) found four faults in rule v4: (1) its ruler is the fight exam's 400 human starts, the held-out reading, now spent as a selection set; (2) the incumbent's stored games are the very games that won its promotion, a lucky draw every later candidate must beat; (3) a candidate with no true gain is promoted about 12 times in 100 (this memo's simulation: 10 at stage 2, 2 more at stage 3); (4) no Act 3 fight is in the ruler while the week is aimed at Act 3, and the Act 3 guard only refuses. The check of this morning shows v4 itself is genuinely better at the Acts 1 and 2 fights; the whole-run judge (10:04Z) still refused its run candidate on floors (+0.49 a run, under the +0.5 minimum). Rule v5 changes the ruler and the bar, not what the trainer does.

## 2. Rule v5 (`PROMOTION_RULE_V5`, `--rule v5|v4|v3`, v5 the default; v4 and v3 stay as undo paths)

**(a, b) The ruler: two parts, fresh starts, the exam held out again.** Built once by `gym_loop.py gate5-set` into `gym-loop/gate5/`; its runs are added to `exam/exclude_runs.json` so the practice never draws them (backup `.pre-gate5.bak`).
- **Part H, human starts of Acts 1 and 2, 500 starts**, drawn by a seeded hash from the loop's human start list (the corpus's four training fifths; the exam fifth is kept out by the list's own build and re-checked by the fixed hash rule; the ids file is never read), only starts whose run the loop never played and that the fight exam did not take (none of the exam's 400), at most one start a run: Act 1 boss 168 (28 per boss), Act 2 boss 162 (54 per boss), Act 2 elite 85, Act 2 hallway 40, Act 1 elite 25, Act 1 hallway 20. The sizes follow the weights below and each cell's measured spread (more starts where differences vary, fewer in hallways we almost always win). The human's own result is kept, so every verdict still carries the human line.
- **Part A3, the two Act 3 bosses, 414 starts**: lane 5's Act 3 exam design (its six cells, boss and turn; its quotas, the Test Subject first: 80 + 40 human openings and 60 + 30 of our own arrivals; Queen and Aeonglass 32 / 20 human and 30 / 20 ours; its builder rules), **drawn a second time from the starts the exam did not take**, so the exam's own 414 Act 3 starts stay a held-out reading like the 400. The clean human pool left outside the exam holds 506 starts across the six cells, enough for the 224. One constant (`act3_source: exam`) makes the gate read the exam's own Act 3 set instead.

**Cell weights: where our runs die.** Within each part a cell counts by our runs' deaths there per 100 runs (the real-game block of generation 20, 2026-11-20, 1,994 runs; non-boss deaths split into elites and hallways by the copy arm's 500 runs):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Act 1 hallway | 2.5 | 3.6% | | first Act 3 boss, Aeonglass | 4.7 | 25.8% |
| Act 1 elite | 2.3 | 3.3% | | first Act 3 boss, Queen | 4.8 | 26.4% |

The Test Subject comes first in the quotas (the most starts, so its own reading is the sharpest and the practice's target is seen best); by deaths per run the three first bosses are now level (4.9, 4.8, 4.7 per 100 runs: after the engine fix and the referee fix the Test Subject passes 12.6 in 100 arrivals in the real game against 19.7 and 20.0), so the weights are level too. Lane 13's re-measure of the Test Subject may change that; the weights are one dict.

**The statistic.** Per start, the mean over its draws of candidate survival minus incumbent survival (survival = won and not capped; HP lost the same way, the whole start HP when lost); per cell the mean over its starts; per part the weighted sum, its standard error the stratified one (each cell's spread of start means over its count, weighted). One-sided bounds: z 1.96 for a promotion (97.5% per route, so the rule as a whole stays near 5 in 100, section 3), z 1.645 for the "clearly worse" guards.

**(d) Stages and routes.** Part H plays draws 0-1, 0-7, 0-15 at stages 1, 2, 3; Part A3 draws 0, 0-3, 0-7 (an Act 3 game costs about as much as an Act 2 boss game, and the part has 414 starts).
- *Guards* (every stage): Part H is clearly worse when its 95% upper bound is under -1.0 point; Part A3 when its 95% upper bound is under -2.0 points (v4's Act 3 guard was -3.0; at -3.0 a candidate 4 points worse at Act 3 and 1 better elsewhere passed 36 times in 100; at -2.0, 11).
- *Stage 1*: reject when either part is clearly worse; else stage 2.
- *Stage 2 and 3, route F (the fights of Acts 1 and 2; the judge's floor route)*: Part H gains at least +0.75 points, its 97.5% lower bound above 0, HP lost there not worse by more than 0.5 a fight, and Part A3 not clearly worse.
- *Route A3 (the Act 3 bosses; the judge's Act 3 route)*: Part A3 gains at least +1.5 points, its 97.5% lower bound above 0, HP lost there not worse by more than 0.5, and Part H not clearly worse.
- *Stage 2 to stage 3* when neither route passed, neither part is clearly worse and Part H gains at least +0.5 or Part A3 at least +1.0; stage 3 is the last. One promotion in 6 hours stays.

**(c) A fresh record for every incumbent.** The draws are numbered by era: in era e Part H plays draws 16e to 16e+15 and Part A3 draws 8e to 8e+7, candidate and incumbent on the same seeds; a promotion starts era e+1. The new incumbent therefore plays its bar on seeds nobody has played, and the draws that selected it are never its bar (v4 stored the winning games as the bar). The incumbent plays its era's draws once, lazily, as the first gate of the era needs them (at most 6.5 CPU-hours an era); its rows are kept in `gate5/games-<name>.jsonl` and identity-checked on an engine change (24 games a part, as v4). The first v5 gate is era 0 with v4 as the incumbent.

**(e) The Act 3 guard is folded into the ruler**: Part A3 is played from stage 1 for every candidate (no separate guard play, no "due" clock), guards route F at every stage, and is itself a route. v4's `act3_guard` code stays for v4.

**Kept from v4**: the paired draws, the CPU prior and `a3-v1`, the identity check, the pre-filter anchor per rule (v5's anchor starts empty), the human line in every stage-2 or stage-3 verdict (now from Part H and Part A3's human starts: a new series; the exam's 400 are read by hand only), no fight-board row for a promotion.

## 3. What the rule does, by simulation (`gate-v5/simulate.py`, 4,000 candidates a line; v4 - v3's measured spread on the exam's 400 starts x 16 draws for Part H and v4's ruler, Bernoulli spreads at the Act 3 exam's kill rates for Part A3; the HP condition taken as met)

|---|---|---|---|

| Acts 1-2 +1.0 point | 70.2 | 62.0 | 4.6 |
| Acts 1-2 +2.0 (v4-like) | 98.5 | 98.8 | 3.4 |
| Act 3 +2.0 | (cannot see it) | 78.6 | 4.1 |

| Acts 1-2 +1.0, Act 3 -4.0 | (guard: about 1 in 3) | 10.6 | 2.8 |

Standard errors: Part H 0.59 points at 8 draws, 0.45 at 16 (v4's exam 0.67 and 0.52); Part A3 0.88 at 4 draws, 0.61 at 8. A no-gain candidate is promoted about half as often as under v4 (6.6 against 12.3; with a 95% bound per route it would be 11); a one-point Acts 1-2 gain is promoted 62 times in 100 (v4 70), a two-point gain as surely as before; Act 3 gains of two or three points, invisible to v4, are promoted 79 and 98 times in 100. Cost per stage (CPU-seconds a game from this morning's rows at 12 processes: Act 1 boss 1.8, Act 2 boss 2.5, elites 1.1-1.3, hallways 0.5-1.4; Act 3 2.1 from a 12-game probe, taken as 2.5): stage 1 0.8 CPU-hours, stage 2 3.2, stage 3 6.5 for the candidate; per candidate 3.3 to 4.6 on average. Per day at the daemon's 120-minute cadence: at most 12 offers, of which the pre-filter passes some; 4 gates a day is about 16 CPU-hours (4 in 100 of the machine's thread-hours), plus at most 6.5 for the incumbent's record after a promotion. At `--gate-procs 4` a stage-2 gate is about 50 minutes of wall time, during which the daemon's 12 practice processes wait (the gate and the practice take turns); `--gate-procs 12` would make it about 17 minutes and give the practice back roughly a third of its day (the orchestrator's call; the restart keeps 4).

## 4. (f) Should the trainer rebase on the incumbent after a promotion? No.

The trainer resumes from its own saved state (`trainer/state.pt`) at every start; `--base` is read only at a cold start (no state) and as the template of the checkpoints' `model.json`; its pre-filter scores each checkpoint against the daemon's current incumbent, read from `state.json`, whatever `--base` says. v4 is the trainer's own checkpoint c0380, so the trainer is already on v4's line (two checkpoints and about 1,200 steps past it by 19:02Z 11-20). Rebasing would throw away the steps since and help only when the incumbent comes from another line (a hand-made fighter) or a measured regression follows a promotion; neither holds. The daemon passes the incumbent's folder as `--base` at each start anyway, so the restart moves the label to v4.

## 6. Departures, what is not verified, undo

- Departures from the brief: Part A3 is a second draw of lane 5's design rather than the exam's own 414 (so the exam stays held out; one constant reverses it); the Test Subject comes first in the quotas, not in the weights (the deaths are level); the 95% bound is per rule (two routes at 97.5% each), not per route.
- Not verified: the Act 3 part's spread and cost come from the Act 3 exam's rates on the old engine and a 12-game probe; the first live gate is the test. Lane 13's Test Subject re-measure is not in the weights. The starts' fidelity is lane 5's (rebuilt openings, no relic counters).
- Undo: stop the daemon and start it with `--rule v4` (or `v3`); `gate5/` and the added exclusions stay (the practice loses 914 starts of about 75,000).
