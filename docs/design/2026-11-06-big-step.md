## For the owner

- **Now:** our player beats the second boss in about 9 of 100 games on our copy of the game (2 a week ago) but has won 1 game in 5,000. Games end at the first boss (31 in 100), on the way to the second (30), at the second (20), and almost all who reach the last act die there.
- **The goal is far:** 10 wins in 100 is what an average human manages at this level. It needs the second boss beaten three times as often and a third of last-act games won. Nothing we can see does that in two weeks; the plan aims at 13 to 18 second-boss wins in 100 by 19 November and the first regular wins.
- **The next big step:** let the player think hard only in the fights that kill it (everything after the first act, every elite and boss), with a quicker way of thinking we test first today; teach it to keep potions and health for the big fights; run the self-teaching rounds on top; judge them together, like re-tuning an engine and timing it once, because our tests cannot see small changes.
- **Machines:** the home PC runs about one teaching round a day. One cheap rented machine (about 13 euro cents an hour, about 34 euros to 19 November) doubles that inside the $50 limit; more do not help, because our own planning time per round runs out first.
- **Buy or rent:** a second-hand PC (AUD 500 to 900) costs more than renting for two weeks but is the only way to test on the real game twice as often; decide on 19 November if the plan goes on.

## 4. The real game, weekly

- **The risk.** From now the loop learns from Acts 2 and 3 on the copy (labels, self-play rows, Act 3 starts), where the copy
  disagrees with the game 4.4 and 10.7 times per 1,000 decisions against 0.66 in Act 1, and 24 in 100 runs entering Act 2 meet a
  disagreement (memo:109-110). A copy error that favours a card, relic or line of play is rewarded by the labels, adopted by the
  prior and compounded each round; at one or two rounds a day a week carries 7 to 14 candidates and perhaps 2 to 4 promotions on
  the copy alone. So far the copy was pessimistic at the Act 2 boss per run (generation 11: 2.36 per 100 on copy blocks, 2.97 on
  the nights' copy arms, 3.40 in the game, keep-v2:207-208) and optimistic per fight (23 against 19 in 100, memo:111-112).
- **The bound.** A route A promotion stands only if the next real block confirms it (DECISIONS:1337, 1343); a claim of +3 per 100
  or more triggers a block by hand, which under section 3's stacks is most promotions; the weekly batch catches the rest within 7
  days. Proposed (orchestrator's call): the weekly batch also plays each other generation promoted since the last block on 500 runs
  (about 1.9 h each at x16 on 12 processes), so a failed check names the generation to go back to.
- **The line, per act, at every real block.** For Acts 1, 2 and 3, on the 200 same-seed copy replays and on the newest player's
  real runs against its copy block arm: runs entering the act, reaching its boss, beating it; deaths by room kind (hallway, elite,
  event, boss); disagreements per 1,000 decisions and the share of entering runs meeting one. **An artefact shows** as the copy's
  gain over the previous generation exceeding the game's by more than 2 SE on Act 2 boss kills per 100 runs: SE about 1.2 (2,000
  real runs a generation, unpaired, 0.9; a 3,000-seed paired copy gain about 0.8, keep-v2:83 scaled), so a copy-only gain above
  about 2.4 per 100 is seen; the 200 replays alone see 4 or more. Or as any Act 2 or 3 stage above the game's by more than 2 SE.
  Then labels and self-play rows past floor 17 are down-weighted and that act's disagreement groups go first to the fidelity
  worker (memo 3.6's rule, "two nights running" read as one block now that blocks are a week apart).
