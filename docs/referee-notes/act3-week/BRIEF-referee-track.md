## Words once

The referee plays the real game and our copy of it side by side, move for move. While they agree, our player chooses the moves. When the copy stops matching the game, the referee has "lost its place" (the row's `lost` field, and a divergence record), and a simple stand-in plays the real game to the end of the fight or the run. A fight the stand-in finishes tells us nothing about our player.

## The fault, in numbers
