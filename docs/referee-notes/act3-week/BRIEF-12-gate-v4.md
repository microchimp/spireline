## Why

The gate (rule v3, `PROMOTION_RULE` in `rl/gym_loop.py`) judges every candidate on the board's 200 Act 1 boss starts x 10 seeds under the judge-equipped search `judge-v0`: 2,000 games, SE about 0.65 points, so it cannot see a gain under about +1.1 points. The newest candidate c0380 beats the incumbent v3 by +1.34 points of survival (SE 0.66) and 0.52 HP a fight (SE 0.19) on the fight exam's 400 human starts x 8 draws under the player's own search `a3-v1` (the search the champion plays whole runs with): real, and invisible to v3. The pre-filter (`PREFILTER`, mode `best`) then anchored on gaps of candidates the blind gate had rejected and passed nothing since 11-17. Rule v4 judges the candidate as it will be played, on the exam, at a resolution that sees about +1 point, and every gate doubles as the human-bar reading.

## Rule v4 (build exactly this; constants in one dict `PROMOTION_RULE_V4`, `--rule v4|v3` on the daemon, v4 the default)

1. **Ruler.** The fight exam's human stratum: `exam/starts-human.jsonl` under `D:/spireline/referee/gym-loop/` (400 starts: act1-hallway 31, act1-elite 31, act1-boss 185, act2-hallway 31, act2-elite 31, act2-boss 91), the exam's own configuration (`fight_exam.DEFAULT_CONFIG` = `a3-v1`, CPU prior, as `human_bar()` plays it), paired draws `j` on the same seeds for candidate and incumbent. Survival = won and not capped (`capped: lost`, as v3). The incumbent's exam games are stored once (`exam/games-<incumbent>.jsonl`; v3's 8 draws exist today in `act3-week/plateau-check/games-v3.jsonl` + `stored-games-v3-from-1109.jsonl`) and reused after an identity check on the current engine (replay 24 stored games; outcome, end HP and `moves_sha` must match, as lane 11 did with 100 of 100), so a gate plays only the candidate. A new engine tag re-plays the identity check, never the whole set unless it fails.
2. **Statistics** (`rl/gym_gate.py`): the paired difference per (start, j), averaged per start, SE clustered on starts (`cluster_mean_se`); one-sided bounds with z 1.645 (95) and z 1.282 (90). HP lost per fight = start HP minus end HP (the whole start HP when lost), the same pairing.
3. **Stage 1:** draws 0 and 1 (800 candidate games). Reject when the 95 in 100 upper bound of the survival gain is under -1.0 points. Otherwise stage 2.
4. **Stage 2:** draws 2 to 7 (3,200 games in all). **Promote** when the survival gain is at least +0.75 points, its 90 in 100 lower bound is above 0, and HP lost is not worse by more than +0.5 a fight (point estimate). When the gain is at least +0.5 and the bar is not reached: stage 3. Otherwise reject. The pooled-trend path of v3 (`pooled`, `pool`) is retired for v4 (keep the function for v3).
5. **Stage 3:** draws 8 to 15 (6,400 games in all), the same promote test; otherwise reject.
6. **Act 3 guard** (from the first gate after the daemon has drawn Act 3 practice starts for 24 hours; `games/segments.jsonl` says when): a fixed subsample of lane 5's Act 3 exam cells (`exam/starts-act3.jsonl`, 414 starts, six cells `act3-boss{1,2}-{73,78,80}`): 120 starts, the Test Subject cells (80) first, chosen once by a seeded draw and written to `exam/starts-act3-gate.jsonl`; one draw; played at stage 2 for the candidate (the incumbent's stored and identity-checked the same way). The candidate must not be worse there by more than the 95 in 100 bound of -3.0 points of survival; reported by cell. Cost about 1 CPU-hour (the exam's games take 14 to 72 s of search each).
7. **Pre-filter:** `mode best` anchors only on gaps of candidates gated under v4 (store the rule name with each verdict; verdicts under v3 do not count toward "best gap so far"). `floor_h` 6 stays. `guard_h` 6 (one promotion in six hours) stays.
8. **Human bar:** every stage-2 or stage-3 verdict writes the human-bar line (candidate and incumbent against `human_survived`, overall and by cell, survival and HP lost) to `gate/verdicts.jsonl` and the loop log; `--human-bar` becomes a no-op under v4 (report-only, as before: nothing of it enters the decision).
9. **After a promotion:** as now (`promote`, `human_bar_adopt`, the lab's whole-run judge through `wiring_tick`; D68 addendum 4). The promoted player's exam games become the stored incumbent games.
10. **Costs to report:** CPU-seconds per game by cell from the gate's own rows; a gate at stage 2 about 2.5 CPU-hours, stage 3 about 5, Act 3 guard about 1.
